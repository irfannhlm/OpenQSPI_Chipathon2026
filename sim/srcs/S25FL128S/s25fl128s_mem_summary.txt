s25fl128s.mem preload map

The flash model still defaults all unlisted bytes to FF.
The following blocks are placed for easy readback tracing:

- Block 0 @000000-00001F: readable paragraph
  Text: TRACE BLOCK A: quick brown fox.

- Block 1 @000100-00011F: symbols and punctuation
  Text: symbols: !@#$%^&*()[]{}<>?/+-=_.

- Block 2 @000200-00021F: numbers and hex-style text
  Text: numbers: 0123456789 and hex ABCD.

These blocks are intentionally short and separated so you can
verify address decoding, burst reads, and byte ordering with
simple waveforms or memory dumps.
